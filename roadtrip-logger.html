🚀 Getting Started

Trip Log stores all your data privately in your browser — no account, no cloud, no sign-up required.

  1. Tap New Trip in the top-right and give your trip a name, start date and odometer reading.
  2. Each time you fill up, go to the Fuel tab and log the stop.
  3. Log meals, accommodation, tolls and other costs under Expenses.
  4. Check your running totals in the stats bar at the top.
  5. Visit Summary for charts and a full cost breakdown.
🗺️ Managing Trips
How do I create a trip?
Tap New Trip in the header. Enter a name (required), start date, and your current odometer reading.
How do I switch between trips?
Use the Active Trip dropdown below the header. All tabs update instantly to show the selected trip's data.
How do I edit a trip?
Select the trip from the dropdown, then tap ✏️ Edit. You can change the name, dates, start odometer, and budget.
Can I set a budget?
Yes — enter an optional Trip Budget ($) when creating or editing a trip. A budget tracker appears in the Summary tab showing how much remains.
💾 Data & Privacy
Where is my data stored?
Entirely in your browser's localStorage — on your device, never on a server. Trip Log has no backend and no internet connection needed once loaded.
Will I lose data if I clear my browser?
Yes. Export a JSON backup regularly from Settings to protect your records.
Does data sync between devices?
Not automatically. Export a backup on one device and import it on the other using Merge or Replace mode.
📱 Install on Your Phone
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
Open in Safari → tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. Opens full-screen without the browser bar.
Android (Chrome)
Open in Chrome → tap three-dot menu → Add to Home Screen. Works like a native app.
Does it work offline?
Yes — once loaded, Trip Log works entirely offline. Log fuel stops in the middle of the outback with no signal.
