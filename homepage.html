Trip Log
The clean, simple companion for traditional fuel vehicles — car, caravan, ute or motorbike

Everything you need to track a petrol or diesel road trip without the clutter. Log every fill-up and expense, watch L/100km update in real time, and stay on budget the whole way.

What's included
  • Fuel stops — location, litres, $/L, odo
  • L/100km badge per fill-up
  • Expenses with custom categories
  • Trip budget & live spend tracker
  • Fuel efficiency bar chart

  • Multiple trips, instant switching
  • Vehicle & tow vehicle profiles
  • Edit & delete any entry
  • Odometer sanity warnings
  • Emergency contacts & insurance
  • CSV export (fuel, expenses, summary)
  • JSON backup & restore
  • Install to home screen

Get started

No install. No account. Open and go.

1
Download the file
One HTML file. About 180 KB. Save it to your phone or computer — that's the whole app.
2
Open in any browser
Safari, Chrome, Firefox. Tap the file or drag it to your browser tab. No install required.
3
Create your trip
Name it, choose vehicle type, set a budget. Ready to log in under 60 seconds.
4
Log every stop
Fuel, charging, expenses. Works deep in the outback with zero bars of signal.
Why LogMyJourney AU

Built different.

🔒
Your data is yours. Full stop.
Everything lives in your browser's local storage. No server ever sees your trips, odometer or costs. Export a JSON backup anytime — you own it completely.
📵
Works in the middle of nowhere.
Nullarbor. Gibb River Road. Outback QLD. Once loaded, both apps run 100% offline. Log that fuel stop at Penong without a single bar of reception.
🇦🇺
Actually Australian.
Kilometres. Dollars. Litres. Dates in dd/mm/yyyy. Built by an Australian who knows the difference between a servo, a roadhouse and a truck stop.
⚡
Ready for the EV transition.
Australia's charging network is growing fast. LogMyJourney AU tracks every session — kWh, price paid, SOC percentage, charger type — so you know exactly what each trip cost.
📊
Numbers that mean something.
L/100km per fill shows if towing is hurting your economy. km/kWh shows if your EV is performing. Lifetime trends show how efficiency changes across every trip you've ever logged.
🆓
Free. No catch. No agenda.
No subscription. No premium tier. No ads. No tracking. There's an optional support button inside the app — nothing more. Because Australian road trippers deserve better tools.
